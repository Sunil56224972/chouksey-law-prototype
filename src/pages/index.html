How we teach

A courtroom habit, built from the first semester.

The programmes look at socio-legal problems from several disciplines. That shapes how every course is taught.

About the department →

  1. Case law analysis

    Judgments are read, briefed and debated in class, so students learn how courts reason, not only what they decided.

  2. Moot court training

    Regular moots build drafting, research and oral advocacy in front of a bench.

  3. Legal aid clinic

    Students work on real questions from the community, under faculty supervision.

  4. Legal literacy camps

    Students take basic rights awareness into villages and neighbourhoods around Bilaspur.

  5. Research & legal writing

    Project assignments and seminars on current legal issues build the habit of writing clearly and citing carefully.
