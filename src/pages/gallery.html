Vidyārambh: day one of the first batch.

The induction programme for newly admitted LLB, BA LLB and B.Com LLB students at Chouksey College of Science and Commerce.

The college opened a new chapter on 18 September 2024 by welcoming its first law students. The day opened with a lamp-lighting and Saraswati vandana, followed by addresses from the dais and a welcome for every guest.

Chief Guest
Shri Nirmal Minz, Retd. Principal Judge
Chief Patron
Dr. Ashish Jaiswal, Managing Director
Venue
Swami Vivekanand Auditorium
