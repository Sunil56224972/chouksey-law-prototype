Three degrees. One profession.

Integrated five-year degrees after Class 12, or the three-year LLB after graduation. All three follow the Bar Council of India's Legal Education Rules, 2008.

Programme I

BA LLB

Bachelor of Arts & Bachelor of Legislative Law

Duration
5 years
Intake
60 seats
Eligibility
10+2, 45%
Annual fee

An integrated course combining arts with law. Students study the social, political, historical and philosophical ideas that law grows out of, alongside a full law curriculum, so they can read a statute against the society it governs.

Indicative areas of study†

  • Political Science
  • Sociology
  • History
  • English
  • Constitutional Law
  • Law of Contract
  • Law of Torts
  • Criminal Law
  • Family Law
  • Jurisprudence
  • Moot Court & Drafting
