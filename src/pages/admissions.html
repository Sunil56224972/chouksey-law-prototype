Process

Four steps to your seat.

Registration and fee payment both happen on the college's online admission portal.

  1. Register online

    Create your application on the admission registration portal.

  2. Keep documents ready

    Mark sheets for Class 10, 12 and graduation (for LLB), transfer and migration certificates, ID proof and photographs.

  3. Verification

    The admission cell checks your eligibility against the university and Bar Council of India norms.

  4. Pay & confirm

    Pay the first-semester fee through the online payment portal to confirm your seat.
