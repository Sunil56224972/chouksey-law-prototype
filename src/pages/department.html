About

About the department

The Department of Law was established in 2024 at Chouksey College of Science and Commerce, Lalkhadan, Bilaspur. The curriculum follows the Legal Education Rules, 2008 of the Bar Council of India.

All three programmes, BA LLB, B.Com LLB and LLB, are full-time. They are built to encourage multi-disciplinary analysis of socio-legal problems, and the course structure and teaching methods follow from that aim.

The college is affiliated to Atal Bihari Vajpayee Vishwavidyalaya, the state university set up under Chhattisgarh Act No. 07 of 2012. The university sits in the Old High Court Building near Gandhi Chowk, Bilaspur.

Method

Lecture is only the opening statement.

The department mixes lecture discussion with clinical work, so students meet both the theory and the practice of law.

  1. Lecture discussions

    Lectures run as discussions, with students expected to question the reading, not just record it.

  2. Case law analysis

    Judgments are briefed and discussed to follow how courts reason.

  3. Moot court

    Students draft memorials and argue before a bench to build advocacy skills.

  4. Legal aid clinic & literacy camps

    Clinical courses take legal knowledge out of the classroom and into the community.

  5. Seminars & project assignments

    Seminars on current legal issues and written projects train students in legal research and writing.
