Undergraduate law · Full-time

Three degrees. One profession.

Integrated five-year degrees after Class 12, or the three-year LLB after graduation. All three follow the Bar Council of India's Legal Education Rules, 2008.

Programme I

BA LLB

Bachelor of Arts & Bachelor of Legislative Law

Duration
5 years
Intake
60 seats
Eligibility
10+2, 45%
Annual fee
₹40,000

An integrated course combining arts with law. Students study the social, political, historical and philosophical ideas that law grows out of, alongside a full law curriculum, so they can read a statute against the society it governs.

Indicative areas of study†

  • Political Science
  • Sociology
  • History
  • English
  • Constitutional Law
  • Law of Contract
  • Law of Torts
  • Criminal Law
  • Family Law
  • Jurisprudence
  • Moot Court & Drafting

5 years
Intake
60 seats

  • Business Economics

  • Taxation

Programme III

LLB

Bachelor of Laws

Duration
3 years
Intake
60 seats
Eligibility
Graduation, 50%
Annual fee
₹30,000

The standard law degree for graduates of any discipline. A concentrated three years cover the core of substantive and procedural law, with moot court, legal aid work and research built into the course.

Indicative areas of study†

  • Constitutional Law
  • Law of Contract
  • Law of Torts
  • Criminal Law
  • Family Law
  • Property Law
  • Law of Evidence
  • Civil & Criminal Procedure
  • Jurisprudence
  • Professional Ethics
  • Moot Court & Drafting
Check eligibility & apply →

Indicative only. The semester-wise syllabus is set by Atal Bihari Vajpayee Vishwavidyalaya under the Bar Council of India's Legal Education Rules, 2008. Duration, intake, eligibility and fees are as published by the college for 2026–27.
